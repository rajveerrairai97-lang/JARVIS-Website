02 / PRIVACY

Your data stays yours.

JARVIS is designed around local-first operation and user control.

🔒

Local-First Architecture

JARVIS is designed to operate locally on your computer. Your reminders, routines and personal information are intended to stay on your machine rather than being uploaded to a JARVIS server.

01

No Account Required

No JARVIS account is required for the basic local reminder system.

02

No Forced Cloud

The core reminder experience does not depend on storing your personal reminders in a cloud database.

03

You Control Your Data

Your local files remain accessible to you, giving you control over your own information.

ⓘ

JARVIS is a local-first project. Always review the code and dependencies yourself before using any software with sensitive information.

A quick note about Windows security

Because JARVIS is a new application, Microsoft Edge or Windows SmartScreen may display a warning saying that the installer isn't commonly downloaded or that Windows protected your PC.

This can happen with new software that has not yet built up download reputation.

Only continue when you downloaded JARVIS_Setup.exe from the official JARVIS website and the file is the installer you expected.

Why does the warning appear?

Microsoft Defender SmartScreen uses reputation and other security signals to help identify potentially unwanted or dangerous downloads. A newly released application may have little or no download reputation yet, so a warning can appear even when the developer is distributing the application legitimately.

As JARVIS becomes more widely downloaded and established, its reputation may change.
